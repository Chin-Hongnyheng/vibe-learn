## Decisions and Assumptions
- **Preserve and extend existing code**: Do not replace or break existing routes or components. Enhance `CourseDetailPage` and mock data.
- **Hero Section CTA**: Update `HeroSection` "Explore Courses" button to use `<Link to="/courses">` so clicking it navigates to `/courses`.
- **Course Card Navigation**: Ensure course cards in all views cleanly route to `/courses/${course.id}`.
- **Course Detail Page Composition**:
  - Follow `docs 2/design/detail-page.png` layout, typography, colors, and components.
  - Breadcrumb: Home icon (Lucide `Home`) > "All Courses" (Link to `/courses`) > Course Title.
  - Course Hero:
    - Cover artwork: High-fidelity Next.js visual card with glowing radial gradient, Next.js logo "N", and "NEXT.JS" label for Next.js course, or course image for other courses.
    - Popular pill badge: `bg-emerald-100 dark:bg-emerald-950/50 text-emerald-700 dark:text-emerald-300 font-bold text-xs uppercase px-3 py-1 rounded-full`.
    - Title: Render with split highlight (e.g. "Next.js for " in text-neutral-900 / dark:text-white and "Production" in text-primary-500 / text-emerald-500).
    - Summary & Meta row: Intermediate (signal icon), 18h 24m (clock icon), 12 modules (file/layers icon), 2.1k students (users icon).
    - Buttons: Primary "Continue Learning ->" with play icon and right arrow, and Secondary "Bookmark" with bookmark icon.
  - "What you'll learn" section:
    - Lightbulb icon + heading.
    - Right-aligned cursive "Build for real world" badge (`font-['Caveat',cursive] text-emerald-500`).
    - 4 outcome cards with soft green icon badges and clean typography.
  - "Course Content" section:
    - Layers icon + "Course Content" + "12 modules • 18h 24m".
    - Numbered items (1 to 12) with module name, description, duration, and chevron.
    - Show/hide toggle ("Show all 12 modules" / "Show less").
  - Floating/Sticky Progress Bar:
    - Fixed at bottom or sticky bottom container with backdrop blur, rounded-2xl, border, showing 35% progress with "Continue Learning" CTA.
  - Fully responsive down to 320px mobile viewports (stacking columns, full-width buttons on mobile).
  - Theme compatibility: Support both light and dark modes cleanly using semantic Tailwind tokens.
